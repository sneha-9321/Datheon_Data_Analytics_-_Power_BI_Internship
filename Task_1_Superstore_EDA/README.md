# Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis on Superstore Retail Sales

**Notebook:** `Task1_Superstore_EDA.ipynb` (run with outputs saved)

**What it covers**
- Cleaning: separated the pasted People and Returns tables, fixed dates and postal codes, checked duplicates, nulls and ranges
- Customer purchase volume, monthly seasonality, category / region / segment profit, discount sensitivity, returns
- Seven business findings and five recommended actions

**Key findings**
1. Sales grew 51% from 2015 to 2018, but average order value fell from about $500 to $435.
2. November and December bring about 30% of annual sales.
3. Furniture is 32% of sales but 6.4% of profit (Tables and Bookcases lose money).
4. Discounts above 20% lost $135K, nearly half of total profit.
5. The Central region and four states (Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Illinois) lost $70.9K combined.
6. 98.5% of customers repeat, but the top customer by sales lost money.
7. About 6% of orders are returned.
